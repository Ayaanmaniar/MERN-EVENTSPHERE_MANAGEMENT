# EventSphere — User Guide

## 1. Getting started
1. Open the site and click **Sign up**.
2. Choose **Attendee / Visitor** or **Exhibitor**, fill in your details, choose a password (8+ characters, with a letter and a number) and tick the consent box.
3. Log in. If you forget your password, click **Forgot password?** on the login page and follow the emailed link (valid 30 minutes).

The top bar shows pages for your role, a 🔔 notification bell and (for chat) an unread-message count.

## 2. Attendees
- **Browse expos** on the home page; search by title, location or theme.
- Open an expo to see **Overview, Schedule, Exhibitors and Floor plan**. Click **Register for this expo** to sign up.
- **Schedule:** *Register* for a session or workshop (capacity is respected) or ☆ *Bookmark* it. Your picks appear under **My Schedule**. You get a notification 15 minutes before a session starts and whenever the organizers change or cancel it.
- **Floor plan:** click any booth to see the exhibitor, products and a *Message exhibitor* button.
- **Exhibitors:** search by keyword/product and filter by category or expo. Open a profile to chat, or email to request an appointment.
- **Messages:** chat with exhibitors and the organizers (support). **Feedback:** send suggestions or report problems.

## 3. Exhibitors
1. **Company Profile** — add logo, description, category, products and contact details.
2. **Applications** — click *Apply* on an open expo, enter company details/products and attach documents (pdf/doc/image/zip, max 5 files × 5 MB).
3. Wait for the organizers' decision (you'll be notified). Once **approved** go to **My Booth**.
4. **My Booth** — click an available (green) booth on the floor plan and *Reserve this booth*. You can hold one booth per expo; *Release* it to choose another. The organizer may also assign you a booth.
5. Fill in **Booth details** (title, products showcased, staff list).
6. **Messages** — talk to organizers, attendees and neighbouring exhibitors.

## 4. Organizers (admin)
- **Dashboard** — live totals (expos, exhibitors, attendees, pending applications, booth visits...).
- **Expos** — create / edit / delete expos. Status: *Draft* (hidden), *Published*, *Completed*.
- **Booths & schedule** (per expo):
  - *Floor plan & booths:* set rows × columns and click **Generate floor plan**. Select a booth to change size/price, **block** it, **assign** it to an approved exhibitor, or **release** it.
  - *Schedule:* add sessions with times, speaker, topic, room, capacity and type. Editing or deleting a session instantly updates everyone viewing the expo and notifies registered attendees.
  - *Attendees:* list of registrations.
- **Applications** — review company details and documents; **Approve** (optionally assigning a booth) or **Reject** with a note.
- **Reports** — attendee engagement, booth occupancy, booth traffic, session popularity; refreshes every 10 s; **Export CSV** or **Print**.
- **Feedback** — reply to users and mark items resolved.

## 5. Your privacy
Go to **My account** (your name in the top bar): update your details, change your password, change marketing consent, **download all your data**, or **delete your account**.
